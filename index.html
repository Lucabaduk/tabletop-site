Features

Everything a small restaurant needs, nothing it does not

Every screen below is a real screenshot of the TableTop demo. Open the live demo and click around.

Menu manager In the app today

Your whole menu, margins included

Add items, group them into categories and set prices. Each item can carry options like "add bacon +$2" or "no onions", and a food cost so you can see your margin at a glance.

  • Categories, prices and add-on options
  • Food cost and margin shown next to every dish
  • One toggle marks a dish sold out (86'd), and it disappears from ordering
POS and receipts In the app today

Take an order in a few taps

Pick dine-in or takeout, choose the table and server, tap dishes and options, add tax and tip, then take card or cash. Every order can print a clean receipt with your restaurant name and address.

  • Dine-in and takeout tickets, several open at once
  • Tip buttons at 15, 18 and 20 percent, or type your own
  • Printable receipts, and order history by day
Kitchen display In the app today

Goodbye, lost paper tickets

Orders appear on the kitchen screen the moment they are sent. Cooks move each ticket from new to cooking to ready to served, and a timer on every card turns amber and then red when food is taking too long.

  • Four clear columns: new, cooking, ready, served
  • Order notes and allergy notes shown right on the ticket
  • Timers that warn you before a table gets upset
Table map In the app today

See your whole floor at once

Every table is a card showing whether it is free, occupied or reserved, what the open ticket is up to and how long the table has been going. Tap a table to open its ticket or start a new order.

  • Free, occupied and reserved at a glance
  • Open the ticket or start an order straight from the table
  • Add and edit tables as your room changes
Reservations In the app today

Know who is coming tonight

Keep bookings with name, phone, party size and table. Filter to today or upcoming, and when the guests arrive tap Seat: the table is marked and their ticket opens.

  • Upcoming, today and all views
  • Seat a party and open their order in one tap
  • Reserved tables show on the floor and dashboard
Dashboard In the app today

Your numbers, without a spreadsheet

Today's sales, open orders, average ticket, best sellers, hourly sales and 30-day trends all sit on one page. Ingredients running low show up as a warning at the top so you reorder before you run out.

  • Sales by hour and the last 30 days
  • Top-selling items and food cost percentage
  • Low-stock alerts from the built-in inventory page

FAQ

Questions owners ask

Does TableTop really work without internet?

Yes. TableTop is a single file that runs in your browser and keeps your data on your own device, so a dropped Wi-Fi connection does not stop service. Try it: open the demo, turn off your Wi-Fi and keep clicking.

Where is my data stored?

Only in the browser on the device you use. Nothing is uploaded. That keeps things private and fast, and it also means the kitchen tablet and the front counter do not share orders yet. Syncing devices is on our roadmap. You can export a backup file at any time from Settings.

How much does it cost?

We plan one flat price of about $25 to $30 a month per restaurant with every feature included, and a free 30-day trial. There are no per-seat or per-feature fees, and tip tools are not an upsell. These are planned launch prices that we will confirm before anyone is ever charged.

What is in the app today and what is coming?

Today: menu manager, point of sale with receipts, kitchen display, table map, reservations, dashboard, inventory, a staff list, PIN lock and backups. Coming in v2: staff schedule, tip splitter, end-of-night report, allergy tags and the staff training quiz. Items marked "Coming in v2" are not in the demo yet.

Can I use it on a tablet or phone?

Yes. The layout adapts to phones and tablets, so you can run the kitchen screen on a cheap tablet and take orders on your phone. Remember that each device keeps its own data for now.

Who is it built for?

Small restaurants and cafes with under 15 staff that find the big systems too complicated or too expensive.
