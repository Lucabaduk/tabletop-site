FAQ

Common questions

Do I need special hardware?

No. TableTop runs in a web browser on the phones, tablets and laptops you already have.

Does TableTop process card payments?

Not yet. You record whether a guest paid by card or cash, and keep using your current card reader.

How does QR ordering work?

Each table gets a QR card. A guest scans it, orders from their phone, and the order appears on your kitchen screen for staff to accept. It needs an internet connection.

Where is my data stored?

Your menu, tables, orders and settings are saved in the browser on each device. Guest QR orders pass through our server only to reach your kitchen. See our privacy page.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. There's no contract and no setup fee.
