Quick answers

Can guests order from their own phone?

Yes. Print a QR card for each table. A guest scans it, sees your menu and orders from their phone. The order goes through a small shared server to your kitchen screen, where staff accept or reject it. It needs an internet connection.

Is the checkout real?

No. It is a practice checkout in test mode. It only accepts the test card shown on the checkout page, no money is charged, and nothing is unlocked by it. The app is open to try right now.
