Try TableTop right here

This is the real app with a sample restaurant called The Copper Spoon. Click around, take an order, send it to the kitchen. Everything you do stays in your browser.

Open full screen ↗← Back to site

Not loading? Open the demo in its own tab. To start over, go to Settings inside the app and choose “Load demo data”.

1. Take an order

Go to POS, pick a table, tap a few dishes and press Card or Cash. Then open Orders to print the receipt.

2. Run the kitchen

Open Kitchen and move a ticket from new to cooking to ready. Notice the timers.

3. Mark a dish sold out

In Menu, flip the switch on any item. It greys out in the POS right away.

4. Check your numbers

The Dashboard shows sales by hour, best sellers and low-stock alerts.
