Privacy

Draft in plain language. This is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

TableTop stores your menu, tables, orders and settings in your own browser on each device. Guest QR orders pass through the TableTop server so they can reach your kitchen screen, and are kept there only as long as needed to deliver them. We do not sell data. If you contact us, we keep your name and email only to reply to you. Ask us to delete it anytime through the contact form.
