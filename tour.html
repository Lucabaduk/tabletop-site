See TableTop at work

This is the real app, starting with a sample restaurant called The Copper Spoon. Click around, take an order, send it to the kitchen. Everything you do stays in your browser, and the tour and the app share the same saved data.

Launch TableTop ↗← Back to site

Not loading? Open the app in its own tab. To reset the sample data, go to Settings inside the app.

1. Take an order

Go to POS, pick a table, tap a few dishes and press Send to kitchen. Later, press Card or Cash to pay, then open Orders to print the receipt.

2. Run the kitchen

Open Kitchen. The order you just sent is in the new column. Press → cooking, then → ready, and notice the timers.

3. Mark a dish sold out

In Menu, flip the switch on any item. It greys out in the POS right away.

4. Check your numbers

The Dashboard shows sales by hour, best sellers and low-stock alerts.

Also in the app Included now

Try Schedule, Tip Split, Night Report and the allergy and diet tags on menu items. They are all in the app menu. Guest QR ordering is live: guests scan a table's QR card and order from their phone, and the order reaches your Kitchen screen through a small shared server.
